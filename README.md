# MinorLeagueCurling
 



## Overview & Game Design

### World & Setting
In the remote Alaskan town of Broomington, curling isn’t just a sport—it’s a way of life. Decades ago, a janitor-turned-legendary sweeper mastered his craft by cleaning everything from trash to oil spills. Then, one day, he vanished.

Every year, Broomington hosts a high-stakes curling tournament, attracting teams from around the world with a lucrative cash prize from a mysterious donor. Unfortunately, the hometown team hasn’t won in decades.

Enter you: a janitor hired as the ‘Floor Manager’ of Broomington’s recreational center. What you didn’t realize? The job also includes leading the town’s curling team. There’s just one problem: you’ve never curled in your life.

You’ll need to assemble a ragtag team of local Broomingtonians, ranging from doctors & schoolteachers to stay-at-home parents, “That Guy,” and more. They may not look like champions, but with a little training (and a lot of luck), they might surprise you.

Winning isn’t just about the prize money: It’s about proving that Broomington’s got more fight left in it than a bar brawl on dollar beer night. In a town where hard work is a way of life, this tournament is a shot at pride, community, and maybe even uncovering the truth behind its long-lost curling legend.

(But it is kind of about the prize money–you could use the proceeds to help those affected by the costly United States Healthcare system. Or not. Up to you!)

### Gameplay Overview
The game revolves around two core loops:

- Curling Matches: A combination of strategic and fast-paced gameplay blending traditional curling mechanics with over-the-top abilities and chaotic modifications.

- Town Activities: RPG-style interactions where you recruit teammates, explore storylines, and upgrade equipment before each match.

#### Curling Matches
Matches follow a 3-round format, with teams throwing 5 stones per round. Points are awarded for stones closest to the ‘bullseye,’ encouraging a mix of offensive and defensive strategies. However, the Broomington Tournament doesn’t follow official rules, and things can get…wild.
Custom Sweepers & Brooms: Before each match you can equip your sweepers with specialized brooms that provide unique powers.


- Stone Abilities: Before throwing a stone, you can give it an ability (e.g., explosive, rocket-powered, or magnetized stones).

- Obstacle Placement: After each turn, you are able to place hazards on the sheet that can impede your opponent and disrupt their turn (e.g., trash, magnets, oil spills). Obstacles remain on the sheet until they are either destroyed or the round ends.

Matches combine turn-based strategy (ability selection & obstacle placement) with real-time action, as you control sweepers mid-match to influence the stone’s trajectory.

#### The Tournament
The tournament has a group stage followed by an 8-team, single-elimination bracket. Matches occur on weekends, with a week in between to prepare, train, and engage in town activities.

#### Town Activities
Between matches, players can:

- Recruit town residents to strengthen their curling team. Residents have a variety of skills and special abilities.
- Build relationships, recruit sponsors, and progress NPC storylines, possibly unlocking additional abilities & equipment.
- Play unofficial curling matches against locals for practice or rewards.
- Upgrade equipment using tournament earnings, or spend that money in other ways that support the community (Want to buy Bob a burrito? Go for it! Want to help pay for a child’s cancer treatment in the American Healthcare system? Good luck!).

Time progresses as you complete activities, leading up to your next weekend match.

### Tone, Mood, & Genre
Minor League: Curling blends the humor & underdog spirit of Dodgeball: A True Underdog Story with RPG elements reminiscent of Pokémon, Persona, & Undertale. The game explores themes of community, purpose, & resilience, delivered with sharp dialogue and lighthearted absurdity.
Structurally, it’s a JRPG with a sports twist, where the curling matches serve as the equivalent of dungeons, while downtime in Broomington mirrors social simulation mechanics.

### Target Audience
Fans of JRPGs (Persona, Yakuza), quirky sports games (Pyre, Rocket League, Backyard Football), and arcade chaos (Mario Kart, NHL Hitz, NFL Blitz).

### Target Length
Estimated 2–5 hours per playthrough, with replayability through team-building, choices, and PvP multiplayer.
