# Characters

### The Janitor (Player Character)
You play the role of The Janitor, who recently moved to Broomington to follow in the footsteps of janitorial legends. The Janitor serves as the team’s Thrower. 

### The Janitorial Assistant (Jane)
Strength: 5/10
Speed: 5/10

One of the first NPCs you meet, this grizzled veteran of the custodial arts teaches you the fundamentals of sweeping mechanics. She’s very invested in Broomington’s curling history and has suspicious levels of knowledge about the Legendary Janitor….

- Recruitment: You can unlock her as a Sweeper, where she proves that sweeping is an artform.
- Gameplay Vibe: Tactile, obstacle-focused sweeper with stamina that leaves a lot to be desired.
🤝 Team Work | Standardizes sweeper stats | Team Rhythmic

### Bartender (unknown)
Strength: 2/10
Speed: 2/10

She runs the local bar, where everyone comes to talk, scheme, and occasionally throw punches over curling debates. She’s your quest guide, nudging you toward new activities, key NPCs, and side quests, all while delivering brutally honest life advice. She’s seen some shit, lad.

- Recruitment: None
- Gameplay Vibe: The mom you never had.
🤝 Team Work | Standardizes sweeper stats | Team Rhythmic

### Stay-at-Home Dad (Oscar)
Strength: 7/10
Speed: 4/10

Operates on instinct & chaos, making bold plays that sometimes work. Go big or (stay at) home.

- Recruitment: Joins the team if you give him a break from parenting for 30 seconds in the park.
- Gameplay Vibe: His skills are variable & unpredictable, depending on how the day is going. 
🎯 Opposition | Debuffs opponent stats | Opponent
💩 Dirty Diaper | Unlocks the "Poopie Diaper" rock if using the Stay-at-Home Mom & Dad duo |Rock Ability

### Stay-at-Home Mom (Stacy)
Strength: 4/10
Speed: 7/10

A parenting powerhouse. What she gives to her child, she gives to your stones.

- Recruitment: She can be recruited as a sweeper, but only if she can find a babysitter. 
- Gameplay Vibe: Tactical, buffs consistency. Shit needs to get done and there isn’t anyone else to do it–she’ll come in clutch during the last turn of a round.
🥌 Hoarder | Gain an extra random rock in inventory | Inventory

### Store Merchant (Bob)
Strength: 3/10
Speed: 3/10

The owner of Broomington’s only general store, selling everything from high-end curling gear to questionable gas station taquitos (which your dog loves). If you need a broom, an upgrade, or something completely useless (but weirdly intriguing), he’s your guy.

- Recruitment: Later in the story, he can be recruited as a sweeper, bringing his merchant instincts to the ice by finding loopholes in the rules and baffling opponents with his skullduggery.
- Gameplay Vibe: Aggressive, manipulative, and intolerant of messy workspaces. He makes up for his relatively lower degree of skill and stamina with a battery of strategic ploys.
🫙 Inventory+ | Bring an additional stone into a round. | Inventory
🥷 Sabotage | Removes one of the opponent’s rock abilities. | Opponent



### Store Vagrant
Strength: 6/10
Speed: 2/10

Living in a tent outside the store, this resourceful scavenger earns a living by recycling bottles and sweeping the pavement outside of the store. His expertise in clearing debris makes him a natural Sweeper, especially when the ice gets cluttered with obstacles.

- Recruitment: Recruiting him might require some unconventional persuasion (or just offering him a very specific brand of canned chili).
- Gameplay Vibe: Chill, slow, methodical, and oddly effective–he does the dirty work.
🔊 Off Beat
Expands timing window for rhythmic sweeping.
Rhythmic


### Hospital Nurse (Joy)
Strength: 4/10
Speed: 6/10

She’s seen it all: curling injuries, bar fights, one very confusing moose-related accident. Not exactly thrilled to be part of the team, but someone’s gotta keep these idiots in one piece.

- Recruitment: She joins your team begrudgingly, but her skills could literally save the game.
- Gameplay Vibe: Healer archetype, reducing the cooldown period due to exhaustion.
🕵️ Spyscope | See your opponent’s equipment before the match. | Inventory



### Restaurant Owner (Charles)
Strength: 2/10
Speed: 3/10

The proud and slightly eccentric owner of ‘Tom’s Trout’, a restaurant that exclusively serves shrimp burritos. If you can endure his passionate seafood monologues, you’ll find he’s actually a solid sweeper with unique team-based abilities when paired with other service workers.

- Recruitment: Recruiting him might require a special burrito-related challenge (don’t ask).
- Gameplay Vibe: Team player, loves shrimp, and thrives as the ‘glue’ of the team.
🚀 RocketRock | Unlocks the Rocket Rock. | Rock Ability
🖐️ Mime | Standardizes strength & speed between sweepers. | Rhythmic



### Restaurant Waiter (Sassana)
Strength: 8/10
Speed: 3/10

She cleans…sometimes. She takes orders… when she feels like it. Somehow, she’s never been fired. No one knows why she’s so perpetually over it, but something tells you she has layers.

- Recruitment: She can be recruited as a sweeper, but good luck getting her to show up on time.
- Gameplay: Takes out her passive aggression on the ice. If she feels worse, she sweeps better. 
😡 Intimidate | Lowers opponent sweeper skills by 1 | Opponent


### Dog (Apollo)
Apollo is your dog, and he is perfect. He may not be able to curl (yet), but he provides moral support, reacts to key story moments, and has an uncanny ability to find hidden items.

- Recruitment: If you feed him a gas station taquito, something weird might happen.
- Gameplay: Apollo loves tennis balls. If you throw a stone with a tennis ball attached…he’ll probably fetch it. 


## Curling Game Announcers
Guiding you through the chaos are Mick “The Moose” Sliderson and Broomy O’Callahan, your ever-bickering announcers who turn every match into a comedic spectacle. 

### Mick “The Moose” Sliderson
Mick “The Moose” Sliderson is an old-school, no-nonsense, curling traditionalist who played back in the day when “sweeping was for survival, not just strategy.” His voice is deep, slow, and deliberate like a man who's seen too many curling stones in his day.

Signature Style: Always skeptical of modern gimmicks, grumbles about rule changes, and tells long-winded curling war stories from his playing days.

- “Back in my day, we didn’t have fancy broom technology—we had mustaches and fear.”
- “Well, that shot was riskier than a moose on black ice.”
- “If you don’t sweep, you don’t deserve to win.”
- “This guy's throwing rocks like he's paying for them by the pound.”

### Broomy O’Callahan
Broomy O’Callahan is an over-the-top showman, prone to dramatic overreactions, questionable curling knowledge, and spontaneous analogies that make no sense. He’s high-energy & fast-talking like he drank six coffees and sprinted to the commentary booth.
Signature Style: Loud, unpredictable, constantly hyping up the drama (even when there is none), and has a bad habit of mispronouncing curling terminology.

- “THAT’S A STONE COLD BEAUTY, MOOSE!”
- “This is shaping up to be the greatest curling match since the Great Broom Riot of ’92!”
- “It’s a CLEAN SWEEP, BABY! This ice has never been shinier!”
- “Have you ever seen a rock move like that? 'Cause I haven’t! And I’ve been watching curling for… well, a few days now.”


### Dynamic Together:
Mick grumbles while Broomy overhypes everything.
Mick corrects Broomy’s inaccurate curling facts, which only makes Broomy double down.
Mick sees curling as an art; Broomy sees it as a full-contact sport waiting to happen.

Example Exchange:
Broomy: “OH-HO! That rock just SLAMMED into the guard like a runaway snowplow!”
Mick: “...It tapped the guard. Barely moved it.”
Broomy: “EXACTLY! A slow-motion disaster! You hate to see it!”
Mick: “You don’t even know what a disaster looks like, kid.”
Broomy: “Oh, trust me, I do—I once saw a man eat a shrimp burrito from Tom’s Trout. Still haven’t recovered.”
