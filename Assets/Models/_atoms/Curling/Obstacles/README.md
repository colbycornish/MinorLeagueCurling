# Obstacles

## Environmental Obstacles
After each throw, players can place obstacles on the ice to disrupt their opponent’s next move. These obstacles persist for the entire round, creating an evolving battlefield that forces strategic adaptation.

Types of Obstacles: Each obstacle affects the stone’s movement in different ways. Some trigger on collision, some trigger based on proximity, and some obstacles are ‘always active.’

### Collision
🚧 Basic Debris | Reduces speed of rock
🚧 Barrier Debris | Blocks stones from passing through.
🛢️ Oil Slick | Causes stones to slide unpredictably.
🗑️ Debris | Slows down stones on impact.
🕳️ Hole | Stones can fall in (teleport or remove).
⏫ Speed Boost | Increases stone speed.
⏬ Speed Bump | Slows stone movement.
💣 Bomb | Explodes, altering stone direction.

### Proximity
🧲 Magnet | Pulls stones toward its location.
🧲 Reverse Magnet | Pushes stones away.
💣 Magnetic Bomb | Explodes when a stone gets too close.
🔃 Rotating Ice | A spinning ice patch that changes the stone’s trajectory depending on when the stone enters.

### Always Active
🎳 Bumper Rails | Just like bowling, these cause the stone to bounce back into the course.


These obstacles add an additional strategic element, forcing players to anticipate opponent movements, counterplay, and unintended chaos.
